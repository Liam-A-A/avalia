What is Avalia

Experience spaces before you commit to them.

Avalia gives event planners, wedding planners, and property buyers a way to truly explore a venue or property in photorealistic 3D — navigating freely, capturing viewpoints, and visualising possibilities — before setting foot inside.

Capture & redesign Snapshot any viewpoint and explore AI-powered room redesigns — see the space your way.
