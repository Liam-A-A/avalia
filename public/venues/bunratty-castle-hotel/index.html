The property

Over a thousand years of welcome on the Shannon estuary.

Bunratty has been a gathering place for over a thousand years. The ground where the hotel stands is said to trace back to a Norse trading camp on the Shannon estuary, the same strategic approach that drew four successive castles to be built, contested, and rebuilt on the land alongside.

The modern hotel sits at the heart of all of it. A four-star property of 144 rooms and suites, twelve kilometres from Shannon Airport and a short stroll from Bunratty Castle and Folk Park, with a spa, leisure facilities, and a long-established reputation for weddings and events. In 1996 it was brought into the Blarney Group, a home-grown Munster success story now in its third generation under the Kelleher family, and a deliberate westward chapter began: a destination hotel for a village that already carried one of Ireland's most recognisable identities.

The 2026 renovation is the most comprehensive since that chapter opened. New guest rooms, a transformed bar in Fibber McGee's, refreshed communal spaces throughout. This is the moment the renewed spaces are captured.
