From first page to finished book

You start the book. Everyone else joins in at their own pace, from their own phone or computer.

  1. Start a book

    Give it a title and tell us who it is for: their name, their age and the things they love right now.

  2. Invite the family

    Send a link to grandparents, aunts, uncles and cousins, near or far. Nobody needs to be good with technology.

  3. Everyone adds a page

    Write a page of your own, or fill in the details of the story you would like the child to hear.

  4. Print it or share it

    Turn the finished book into pages you can print, or send it to the child and anyone else who should have it.

Two ways to add to a book

Write a page

For the writers in the family. Tell part of the story in your own words, as long or as short as you like.

Fionn's great-grandad kept bees behind the old school. He told them everything, and he said the bees always listened.

So when Fionn had a secret, he whispered it to the hive.

Tell us what they love

For everyone else. Answer a few simple questions about the story the child would ask for, and the book holds your answers as notes for the story.

Favourite animal
Hedgehogs
A place they love
Nana's garden
Bedtime ritual
Three songs, then the light off
Something that makes them laugh
Daddy's silly voices
