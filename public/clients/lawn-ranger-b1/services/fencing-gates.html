How we do it

Free site visit and quote

We measure the run, agree the style and give a fixed price.

Clear the line

Old fencing removed and the line set out.

Set the posts

Posts dug in and concreted solid and plumb.

Panels and gates

Panels or rails fitted, gates hung with quality furniture.

Finish and tidy

Everything levelled, treated where needed, and the site cleared.
