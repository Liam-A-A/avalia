Garden Design & Makeovers

Tired garden to finished, paving, raised beds, planting, gravel and lawns, all in one job.

Get a free quote

The LawnRanger takes on complete garden transformations across Tipperary, Limerick and Clare. We combine paving, raised beds, gravel, planting, fencing and new lawns into one finished garden, managed start to finish by one team.

Whether it is a full redesign or reworking one area, we plan it with you, do the hard and soft landscaping, and leave it clean and ready to enjoy.

At a glance

  • Full garden redesigns
  • Raised beds & sleeper features
  • Gravel areas with membrane
  • Planting, soil & new lawns
  • Paving and fencing combined

How we do it

Consult and plan

We walk the garden with you, agree the look and give a fixed price.

Clear the site

Hard landscaping

Soft landscaping

Finish and walkthrough
